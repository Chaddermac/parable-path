# ParablePath Project Brief

ParablePath: Find Your Room is a guided spiritual reflection tool for the House of Stories doctoral project. It helps users discover a possible Broken Story room, the parable that opens the door, the True Story Jesus reveals, the redemptive calling, and one next faithful step.

The movement is **Recognize → Receive → Become**.

## Guardrails

- This is not a personality test.
- This is not a clinical diagnosis.
- This is not prophecy.
- This is not counseling or crisis care.
- The result is a reflection aid, not a fixed label.
- Use tentative language such as “You may be inhabiting…” or “This may reflect…”
- Do not speak as God.
- Do not invent new rooms, parables, or redemptive callings.

## Typology

| Room | False Story | Parable doorway | True Story | Redemptive Calling | Metanoia Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lost | I do not belong. | Lost Sheep, Lost Coin, Lost Sons — Luke 15 | You are sought, found, and welcomed home. | Heavenly Host | Where has absence or shame become an identity, and how might being welcomed form me into a Heavenly Host? |
| Scarcity | There will never be enough. | Rich Fool — Luke 12; Laborers — Matthew 20 | Life is received as gift, not secured by accumulation. | Planetary Trustee | What am I storing, comparing, or controlling because I do not trust that life is gift? |
| Control | I must stay in control. | Unforgiving Servant — Matthew 18; Pharisee and Tax Collector — Luke 18 | Grace frees us to forgive and release control. | Healer | What debt, judgment, or comparison has become part of the architecture of my identity? |
| Stalled | I am not ready yet. | Talents — Matthew 25; Growing Seed — Mark 4 | Faithfulness means participating before everything is clear. | Mentor & Coach | What gift or next step have I buried while waiting to feel fully ready? |
| Boundary | Some people are outside God’s concern. | Good Samaritan — Luke 10; Great Banquet — Luke 14 | Neighbor-love crosses the lines fear tries to protect. | Bridger | Whom has my current story placed outside the reach of my responsibility, table, or compassion? |
| Settling | This is all life can be. | Mustard Seed, Leaven, Hidden Treasure — Matthew 13 | God’s kingdom awakens possibilities we stopped imagining. | Innovator & Imagineer | What kingdom possibility have I stopped imagining because I am protecting an arrangement that feels safe? |
| Delay | I will respond later. | Wise Virgins — Matthew 25; Two Sons — Matthew 21 | God’s kingdom calls for faithful response today. | WayMaker | What faithful response am I postponing, and what path could today’s yes open for others? |
| Distraction | My attention belongs everywhere else. | Sower — Luke 8; Hidden Treasure — Matthew 13 | Attention is discipleship; what we notice shapes who we become. | Awakener | What repeatedly captures my attention, and how might disciplined noticing form me into an Awakener? |
